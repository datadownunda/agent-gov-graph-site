About the project

Evidence first. Conclusions within bounds.

Agent Gov Graph is an open-source research project exploring independent assurance of AI-agent controls. A governance decision is not execution. Execution is not a target-system outcome. Each relationship needs evidence, and missing evidence must remain visible.

Research status: Live positive control-effectiveness attestation has not been demonstrated. Bounded experiments and synthetic decision-rule tests do not establish production assurance or population-wide control effectiveness.

Architecture

Six questions. One evidence chain.

The diagram above is the conceptual research model. AGG examines preserved authority, governance decisions and execution alongside target outcomes and independent evidence to determine which findings are supportable. Its read-only evidence approach aims to work with existing systems; sufficient native evidence cannot be assumed.

Correlation is itself a claim. Ambiguous linkage, incomplete observation coverage and unsupported source semantics constrain what can be concluded.

Research Collaborations

Agent Gov Graph is exploring collaborations with organizations developing realistic AI-agent environments, particularly those involving regulated financial-services workflows.

Our research examines whether independently generated evidence from agent runtimes, governance systems and target applications can be correlated and reconciled to determine what can be established about control effectiveness.

We are interested in collaborating on bounded experiments using existing system-generated evidence, repeatable workflows and independently maintained ground truth.

For RL environment developers, frontier AI research teams and regulated-enterprise technology partners, a joint experiment could compare evidence-based findings with an independently maintained reference outcome. This could complement partners’ existing evaluation and verification work, while testing AGG’s own assumptions about source independence, correlation and evidence sufficiency.
